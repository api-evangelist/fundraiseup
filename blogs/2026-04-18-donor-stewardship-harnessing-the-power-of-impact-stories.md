The foundation for effective fundraising is building meaningful relationships with donors. The most successful nonprofits know that donor stewardship is how to nurture those relationships. Donor stewardship is a long-term strategy that replaces a transactional mindset between donor and nonprofit with a mutually beneficial relationship.
