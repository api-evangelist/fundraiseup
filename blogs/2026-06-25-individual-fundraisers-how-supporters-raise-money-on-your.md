Individual fundraisers are supporters who create their own personal fundraising pages and raise money for your nonprofit from their friends, family, and coworkers. Each person tells their own story, sets a goal, and shares their page, while every gift rolls up into your central campaign. It is the building block of peer-to-peer fundraising , and it is one of the most effective ways to bring in donors you would never reach on your own.
