Donors are increasingly using ChatGPT, Claude and others to discover causes and help them decide where they should donate their money. Your donor can now discover your organization, understand your work, and complete a gift directly inside the AI conversation. The AI landscape is ever-evolving, and after years of leveraging AI to help nonprofits raise money, we have turned our learnings into a powerful new tool called Agentic Giving so nonprofits can participate and benefit from giving through AI channels.
