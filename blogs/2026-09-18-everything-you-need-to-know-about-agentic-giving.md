Every person who currently gives to your nonprofit organization was introduced to you by a friend, an acquaintance, an event, or a simple internet search. Maybe they gave in response to a disaster, an advertisement, or simply because they were looking for someone who aligned with their values. Whatever the reason for giving, they sought you out.
