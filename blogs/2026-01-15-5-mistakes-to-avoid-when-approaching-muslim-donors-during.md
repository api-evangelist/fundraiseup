The Muslim community is one of the most charitable in the United States, with Muslim Americans giving an average of $3,241 annually — significantly more than the general population's average of $1,905. And during Ramadan, giving skyrockets. However, reaching Muslim donors is more of an art, requiring intention, timing, and cultural fluency.
