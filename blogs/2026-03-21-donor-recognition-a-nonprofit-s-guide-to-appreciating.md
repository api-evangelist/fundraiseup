In a world eager for positive change, a boost in donations can be very exciting for nonprofits. Yet, the initial burst of joy soon gives way to a critical insight: securing donations marks only the beginning of a donor's journey. The real challenge lies in not just drawing in these contributors but also in holding their interest through impactful engagement.
