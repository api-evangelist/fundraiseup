If your nonprofit is struggling to hit annual revenue goals or your team feels overwhelmed by the sheer number of ways to solicit donations, then you need a fundraising strategy pronto. Many organizations quickly realize that relying on sporadic campaigns, reactive grant writing, or ad-hoc donor appeals isn’t enough to sustain long-term community impact. What you need is a clear, actionable roadmap.
