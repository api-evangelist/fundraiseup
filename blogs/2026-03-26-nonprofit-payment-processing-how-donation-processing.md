Fundraising technology has exponentially enhanced the way people interact with organizations. The good news is that today’s consumer experience is now personalized down to every delicate detail, however this also means that nonprofits are not matching these consumer experiences, leading to not-so-great donor experiences. An effective way to easily improve the donation experience is to offer a diverse suite of donation payment options.
